# RSA Encryption and Decryption Examples

### Example 1:
#### 1. **Generate Keys:**
- Choose primes:  
  \( p = 5, q = 11 \)
- Calculate \( n = p \times q = 5 \times 11 = 55 \)
- Calculate \( \phi(n) = (p-1) \times (q-1) = 4 \times 10 = 40 \)
- Choose \( e = 3 \) (must be coprime with 40)
- Calculate \( d \):  
  \( 3 \times d \equiv 1 \mod 40 \)  
  By trial: \( d = 27 \)
- Public Key: \( (e = 3, n = 55) \)
- Private Key: \( (d = 27, n = 55) \)

#### 2. **Data:**
- Message: "H"
- Corresponding Number: 7 (H = 7)

#### 3. **Encryption:**
- Formula: \( \text{cipher} = \text{plain}^e \mod n \)
- \( \text{cipher} = 7^3 \mod 55 = 343 \mod 55 = 13 \)
- Encrypted Message: 13

#### 4. **Decryption:**
- Formula: \( \text{plain} = \text{cipher}^d \mod n \)
- \( \text{plain} = 13^{27} \mod 55 = 7 \)
- Decrypted Message: "H"

#### 5. **Verification:**
- Initial: "H"  
- After Decryption: "H"  
- Result: **True**

### Example 3:
#### 1. **Generate Keys:**
- Choose primes:  
  \( p = 11, q = 17 \)
- Calculate \( n = p \times q = 11 \times 17 = 187 \)
- Calculate \( \phi(n) = (p-1) \times (q-1) = 10 \times 16 = 160 \)
- Choose \( e = 7 \) (must be coprime with 160)
- Calculate \( d \):  
  \( 7 \times d \equiv 1 \mod 160 \)  
  By trial: \( d = 23 \)
- Public Key: \( (e = 7, n = 187) \)
- Private Key: \( (d = 23, n = 187) \)

#### 2. **Data:**
- Message: "K"
- Corresponding Number: 10 (K = 10)

#### 3. **Encryption:**
- Formula: \( \text{cipher} = \text{plain}^e \mod n \)
- \( \text{cipher} = 10^7 \mod 187 = 10000000 \mod 187 = 116 \)
- Encrypted Message: 116

#### 4. **Decryption:**
- Formula: \( \text{plain} = \text{cipher}^d \mod n \)
- \( \text{plain} = 116^{23} \mod 187 = 10 \)
- Decrypted Message: "K"

#### 5. **Verification:**
- Initial: "K"  
- After Decryption: "K"  
- Result: **True**
